Briefing

Diez Negritos is an editorial design project focused on the reinterpretation of the cover of Agatha Christie's novel. It provides a contemporary visual solution based on digital illustration, photomontage and layout of flaps, spine and back cover, constructing an atmosphere of mystery coherent with the tone of the work.
