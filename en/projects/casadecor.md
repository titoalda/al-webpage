SIGNAGE SYSTEM DESIGN PROJECT

CASA DECOR

#### SIGNAGE

Construction of the pictographic language based on the corporate typography of Casa Decor, and subsequent design of the signs based on the previously defined proposal. Pictograms and signs designed in Illustrator.

#### SIGNAGE SYSTEM SET

Adaptation of the designed signage proposal to the different elements of the entire signage system (directory, technical data sheets, promotional poster). Signage system designed in Illustrator and prepared as final artwork in InDesign.

[Signage Proposal](https://aldaraalvarez.es/static/files/casadecor_manual-sistema-senaletico.pdf)

## *Briefing*

**Casa Decor** is a corporate identity and signage project for a large-format interior design exhibition event. It addresses the visual unification of the brand and the construction of a signaling system adapted to the installations, collected in detail in a style manual.
