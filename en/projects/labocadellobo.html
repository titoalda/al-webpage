Briefing

La boca del lobo is a lettering design and art direction project focused on the creation of a vinyl record cover (LP 12") for the album of Galician musician Carlos Ares. It consists of developing a visual proposal that captures the raw, cold and bizarre atmosphere of the artist through a contemporary reinterpretation of classic gothic calligraphy (Textura and Fraktur styles). The work ranges from the compositional exploration and geometric mutation of the glyphs —intrinsically integrating morphological features of the wolf such as claws and fangs into the letters— to the final negative digitalization, applying metallic finishes and wear textures to give the piece a visceral and volumetric character.
