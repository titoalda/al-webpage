#### MODEL DESIGN

Design of a house model inspired by children's stories in a *lowpoly* style. Model designed in Procreate.

#### 3D MODELING AND RENDERS

Composition and construction of the geometry of the previously designed model, followed by texture creation and application, lighting and finally 3D rendering. Modeling and renders done in 3dsMax.

[3D Model](https://aldaraalvarez.es/en/projects/casacuentos/visor3D)

## *Briefing*

**Casa de Cuentos** is an interactive 3D modeling project consisting of the modeling, texturing and lighting of a three-dimensional scene inspired by children's stories. The modeling was done following a clean and optimized low-polygon (*lowpoly*) style, with careful work on the lighting and camera layout.
