Briefing

El Viejo y el Mar is an editorial design and illustration project consisting of the complete edition of Ernest Hemingway's book based on the texts from the Debolsillo edition (Penguin Random House). The work ranges from the creation of the graphic identity and cover and interior illustrations to the full layout of the volume, defining the cover, back cover, spine and flaps structure under a single conceptual line.
