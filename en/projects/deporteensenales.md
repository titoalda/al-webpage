DEPORTE EN SEÑALES

#### SIGNAGE

Study of the necessary signs, and subsequent construction of the pictographic language and composition of the signs based on the previously defined proposal. Pictograms and signs designed in Illustrator.

#### SIGNAGE SYSTEM SET

Adaptation of the designed signage proposal to the directory and to the different spaces in the sports complex. Signage system designed in Illustrator and prepared as final artwork in InDesign.

[Signage Proposal](https://aldaraalvarez.es/static/files/deporteensenales_manual-sistema-senaletico.pdf)

## *Briefing*

**Deporte en señales** is a signage design project for a municipal sports complex. It consists of the definition of a clear, direct and legible orientation and spatial identity system to optimize movement through the facilities, reflecting all the graphic and normative development cleanly in a signage manual.
