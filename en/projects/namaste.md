# NAMASTÉ

## BRAND IDENTITY AND PACKAGING DESIGN PROJECT

- SOFTWARE USED
- ILLUSTRATOR
- AUTOCAD
- INDESIGN

BRAND IDENTITY AND PACKAGING DESIGN PROJECT

NAMASTÉ

#### CORPORATE IDENTITY

Creation of all corporate symbology for the Namasté brand (imagetype, logo, isotype, colors and typographies) and its corresponding corporate identity manual. Symbology designed in Illustrator, and manual formatted in InDesign.

#### PACKAGING

Packaging design for the Namasté brand and preparation of its production-ready final artwork. Packaging designed in AutoCAD, and final artwork prepared in InDesign.

[Brand Identity Manual](https://aldaraalvarez.es/static/files/namaste_manual-identidad-corporativa.pdf)

## *Briefing*

**Namasté** is a project of brand identity creation (branding) and packaging design for a line of organic herbal teas. The work consisted of developing from the basic symbology of the brand to the volumetric and graphic design of its packaging, along with the layout of its corresponding corporate identity manual in a clean and structured way.
