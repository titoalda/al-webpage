About me

My
story

I am a graphic designer specializing in brand identity and editorial design, focused on creating projects with a clear purpose and careful design.

Scroll to continue
01

Origin

I was born in Baiona, a town in Galicia surrounded by the sea and nature that has undoubtedly shaped my way of observing the world. That sensitivity to the environment led me to explore visual communication and to understand the importance of detail. Today, I enjoy connecting the tangible world of paper with the endless possibilities of the screen, always seeking that each project has strong roots and its own narrative.

02

Education

Graduated in Design from UCM and with a Master's in Digital Graphic Design from UNIR. My main focus is editorial design and corporate identity, tools that I enhance through illustration, vector work and photo retouching.

  • Essential tools: Adobe InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma.
  • Final artwork: Preparation of files for print, color management and digital media.
  • Methodology: Brand research, conceptualization and creation of adaptable visual systems.
03

Philosophy

For me, creativity and problem solving always go hand in hand. I understand design as a bridge between strategy and aesthetics, and I am passionate about keeping it in constant evolution. I am motivated to keep up with new technologies and digital tools, integrating the latest trends to transform complex concepts into fresh, modern and purposeful visual solutions.
